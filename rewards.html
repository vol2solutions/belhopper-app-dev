🏆 Rewards Model

📊 Tier System

  • 🟦 Blue: 0 – 19 Points
  • 🥉 Bronze: 20 – 39 Points
  • 🥈 Silver: 40 – 69 Points
  • 🥇 Gold: 70 – 89 Points
  • 💎 Elite: 90+ Points

🎁 Redemption Benefits

  • Blue: No redemptions available
  • Bronze: R5 off internal ride OR R10 off external ride [COST: 10 Points]
  • Silver: R10 off internal ride OR R15 off external ride [COST: 15 Points]
  • Gold: R15 off internal ride OR R20 off external ride [COST: 20 Points]
  • Elite: 2 free rides OR R30 off external ride [COST: 25 Points]

⚠️ Limited to 2 redemptions per month per client

🎁 BelHopper Rewards

Earn rewards every time you ride with BelHopper 🚖

For every completed ride request made through the BelHopper app, you earn 1 Reward Point.

The more you ride, the more points you collect — and exciting loyalty rewards, discounts and future ride specials are coming soon.
